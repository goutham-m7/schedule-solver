## Scale curves

```sh
uv run schedule-solver benchmark --sizes 200 500 --families independent ring --budgets 0.1 10 60 --seed 7 --output artifacts/v02-final-curves
```

All inputs synthetic. [Full table](v0.2-curves.md); [metrics, objective passes/bounds, command and runtime metadata](v0.2-curves.json). All 24 generated greedy/optimized schedules were separately parsed as Workload/Result models and passed `validate_schedule()` hard-constraint checking.

At 200 people / independent / budget 60: 400 meetings, priority 794 proven optimal, 7.000s; greedy 320 meetings, priority 714, 0.668s. At 500 people / ring / budget 60: 805 versus 800 meetings, priority 1783 versus 1780, 10.762s versus 5.202s; the optimized priority was FEASIBLE with upper bound 1980, not proven optimal. All 500-person budget-0.1 and budget-10 runs used the labelled greedy fallback.

Search budgets exclude construction and greedy warm-start time. No external calendars, real users or production services were benchmarked.
